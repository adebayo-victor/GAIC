SmartHealth AI: Localized Clinical Assistant

A voice-first, multilingual clinical chatbot engineered for women across Sub-Saharan Africa who lack access to approved medical care.

Project Objectives

Aims of the Project

Bridging clinical divides by delivering specialized, gender-informed healthcare intelligence directly to women.

📍

Democratize Medical Access

Provide women in remote, rural, and underserved areas with immediate, reliable access to approved clinical advice where physical health centers are scarce.

⚖️

Address Historical Healthcare Neglect

Actively counter systemic underrepresentation and neglect of female health by creating a dedicated, safe digital clinical space centered on women’s wellness.

🧬

Gender-Differentiated Symptom Analysis

Bridge the physiological gap by recognizing how biological differences between male and female systems result in distinct manifestations of ailments.

🗣️

Overcome Language & Literacy Barriers

Deliver approved medical insights natively in local spoken dialects, ensuring lack of English literacy never blocks a woman from clinical care.

Decoupled Lifecycle Mandate

Isolating the STT (Ear), TTS (Voice), and Database (Memory) eliminates cross-module lock-in.

Technical Enhancements

  • • Standardized Interface Contracts: JSON schemas guarantee that swapping out an STT model never breaks text parsing.
  • • Asynchronous A/B Testing: Validate dialect model performance live in the field against baseline benchmarks.
  • • Edge Tiering: Heavy LLM reasoning on cloud/edge, with quantized speech models on local clinic tablets.
Module Evolution 02

Dialect & Accent Adaptability (STT Ear)

Training the speech ear independently to decode rural accents, diverse speaking rates, and mixed local phrasing.

👂

Acoustic & Dialect Objective

Enable women in rural areas to speak naturally in Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba, or Hausa without altering their speech patterns.

Decoding noisy rural audio & code-switched dialects into clean text.
⚙️

Technical Enhancements

  • • Code-Switching Support: Accurately parses conversations mixing native vernacular with English medical terms.
  • • Noise-Resilient Decoders: Fine-tuned with outdoor clinic and market chatter backgrounds.
  • • LoRA Fine-Tuning: Low-Rank Adaptation of open Whisper & MMS backbones using grassroots recordings.
Module Evolution 03

Localized Speech Enhancement (TTS Voice)

Refining text-to-speech with regional voice corpora to ensure natural bedside cadence and culturally familiar tones.

🔊

Native Spoken Clarity Objective

Preserve vital tonal inflection so clinical advice is comforting, authentic, and free from misleading acoustic artifacts.
