Women Genital & Fertility Health Foundation (WOGAFH)

Smart Health Community AI

A voice-first, dialect-aware reproductive clinical assistant built for community health workers, traditional birth attendants (TBAs), and rural women across Nigeria in Nigerian Pidgin and Hausa.

Project Scope & Objectives

Core Aims of the Pilot

Bridging the reproductive healthcare gap for underserved communities that cannot express themselves in English.

🩺

Reproductive & Fertility Health Access

Provide private, confidential, and medically vetted clinical guidance on reproductive conditions, menstrual health, and maternal wellness directly to rural women.

🤝

Empower Frontline Caregivers

Equip local Community Health Extension Workers (CHEWs) and Traditional Birth Attendants (TBAs) with rapid diagnostic lookup tools in their local dialects.

🧬

Gender-Differentiated Diagnostics

Counter the systemic neglect of female health by recognizing how ailments manifest uniquely in female reproductive physiology, preventing critical misdiagnoses.

🗣️

Overcome Language & Literacy Barriers

Prioritize Nigerian Pidgin and Hausa for speech and text so that no woman is denied life-saving clinical insights due to an inability to speak formal English.

Module Evaluation · Empirical Benchmark

Central LLM Selection: The Case for Gemini API

Why our engineering bench systematically evaluated Meditron-7B, Qwen-3B, and Nova Lite—and settled on Google Gemini API.

⚠️

Why Alternative Models Fell Short

  • • Meditron-7B: 213.0s CPU latency (unusable for live chat), 14GB disk hog, dry English textbook tone.
  • • Qwen-2.5-3B: Suffered prompt-echoing in Hausa and pirate tone ("Ahoy!") in Pidgin.
  • • Amazon Nova Lite: Fast (1.52s), but had the ìṣèjọ́bà ("government") hallucination in Yorùbá.
🏆

Why Google Gemini API Won

  • • Accra AI Lab Heritage: Pre-trained on West African corpora; speaks natural Pidgin, Hausa, Yorùbá, and Igbo.
  • • 1.20s Latency: Over 70× faster than local CPU models with 0 GB of disk consumption.
  • • 1M+ Token Context: Ingests full chapters of the 2016/2022 Nigerian Treatment Guidelines directly.

Doctor’s Feedback & Triage Guardrails

"AI must act as a safe triage guide, not an autonomous hospital prescriber."

  • • Block Autonomous Prescriptions: The LLM must not prescribe systemic IV/oral antibiotics over chat; it must provide safe first-aid (Paracetamol/ORS) and mandate clinic referral.
  • • Bedside Empathy: Maintain a calming tone to prevent unnecessary patient panic when discussing oncological/obstetric red flags.
  • • Differential Hierarchy: Prioritize statistical commonalities (e.g. threatened miscarriage) alongside emergencies (ectopic pregnancy).
Acoustic Benchmark · STT "Ear"

Speech-to-Text: Meta MMS vs. OpenAI Whisper

Empirical evaluation on native African speech corpora: Google FLEURS Hausa (ha_ng) and open Nigerian Pidgin (pcm).

🎙️

Acoustic Benchmark Scorecard

Hausa WER: Meta MMS-1B (`hau`) 21.74% 🏆 (High Accuracy)
Hausa WER: OpenAI Whisper (Small) 95.65% ❌ (Phonetic Gibberish)
Pidgin WER: Meta MMS-1B (`pcm`) 64.29% 🏆 (Native Phonetics)
Pidgin WER: OpenAI Whisper (Small) 85.71% ❌ (Anglicized Drift)

Base Whisper suffered from an Anglicization bias, forcefully rewriting Pidgin into formal British English while hallucinating words ("an arromba").

⚙️

STT Engineering & Adaptation Strategy

  • • Selected Backbone: Meta MMS-1B with modular language-specific adapters (hau and pcm).
  • • PEFT Adapter Fine-Tuning: Freeze the 1B Wav2Vec2 encoder and fine-tune only the lightweight 9MB adapter layer (adapter.pcm.safetensors) on local clinic audio (<30 mins on T4 GPU).
  • • Inference Optimization: Export MMS to an INT8 / ONNX runtime to drop CPU latency from ~29s down to sub-second (<0.5s) execution on cloud instances.

System Architecture · 5-Layer Blueprint

Master Production Pipeline & Deployment

The complete, decoupled compound AI system engineered for low-bandwidth primary healthcare delivery across Nigeria.

1. Engines

Model Selection

Meta MMS-1B (STT), Meta MMS-TTS (Voice), all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (RAG), Qwen via LiteLLM Bedrock Gateway, Meta NLLB-200 (Bridge).

2. Bridges

Integration Layer

Code for Africa LiteLLM Gateway proxy (litellm.codeforafrica.org) and decoupled FastAPI microservices on Render.

3. Knowledge

Cognitive Core

Full 2016/2022 Nigerian STG corpus indexed in vector memory; doctor-approved prompt bounding first aid and mandatory clinic referral.

4. Safety

Guardrail & Handover

Sub-5ms regex filter intercepts acute emergencies (PPH, eclampsia) and triggers immediate Health Center Referral Cards.

5. Delivery

Multi-Platform UI

Telegram Bot (Telethon), Mobile App, and Web Portal; PostgreSQL anonymized query logging in compliance with NDPR data protection.
